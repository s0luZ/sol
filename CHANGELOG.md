# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Control Centre grows out of the notch in one motion. Drag the notch down and it follows the pointer; a short pull springs back.
- The clock uses the notch’s light text. Clicking it opens a calendar.
- Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Bluetooth, sound, and battery switch popups on the first click. The tray opens right away.
- Night Light sits under brightness: turn the blue light filter on and set how warm it is.
- Show Desktop is a thin strip at the end of the dock.
- Quick actions cover File Explorer, Task Manager, Display, Night Light settings, Snipping Tool, Notifications, Do Not Disturb, Lock, Sleep, Restart, and Shut down. Sleep, restart, and shut down ask first.
- Gaming mode, on by default, keeps the notch and dock hidden in fullscreen and does not pull an app out of it.
- Logs in `%AppData%\SolDesktop` rotate when they get large.

## 0.3.3

- The top notch morphs into Control Centre instead of stacking two bars.
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, sound, Ethernet, and battery on the notch open their own popups.
- Tray overflow lives on the notch and lists live Windows notify icons. Right-click one to quit it.
- Dock folders open a centered Sol explorer.
- Pressing Win raises the dock so Start no longer leaves a gap at the bottom.

## 0.3.2

- Live notch for time, network, Bluetooth, volume, and battery.
- Pull-down Control Centre for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Ethernet, sound, and brightness.
- Dock with window previews, pin, and magnification.
- Dark and Glass themes.
- Start menu centred while the dock is on.
